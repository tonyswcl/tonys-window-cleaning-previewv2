The two minute check

Painted sits on the glass. Etched is in it.

Painted, marker, sticker

  • On top of the glass surface
  • Lifts completely with the right solvent and a blade on wet glass
  • Most Victorville tags are this
  • From $129, usually under an hour

Etched or scribed

  • Acid cream or a scratch tool, cut below the surface
  • No solvent reaches it
  • Shallow marks can be polished, deep ones need the pane replaced
  • $89 assessment, credited if you go ahead

We check this before anything is quoted as a removal. If it is etched you get a written finding and a photo, not an invoice for scrubbing that was never going to work.

FAQ

Graffiti questions from Victorville

How much does graffiti removal cost in Victorville?

A single tag on one pane starts at $129. A storefront with up to six affected panes starts at $289. Same day emergency call out adds $75. If the mark turns out to be etched into the glass, that is a different job that starts with an $89 assessment, credited if you go ahead with polishing.

How fast can you get to Old Town or Seventh Street?

Usually the same day. We are based in Hesperia, ten minutes from Old Town, and graffiti jumps the queue because every day it stays up the odds of a second tag go up.

Does the City of Victorville make me remove it?

Victorville runs a graffiti abatement program and expects property owners to clear tags promptly. Dated before and after photos are what show you acted. We photograph every job and can issue a written report if you need one for the city or an insurer.

Is it painted on or scratched in?

That is the question. Spray paint, marker and stickers sit on the glass and come off completely. Acid etch cream and scribe marks are cut into the glass and no solvent reaches them. We check in the first two minutes on site and tell you which you have before quoting.

Will removal damage window tint?

It can, so we check for film first. Solvent strong enough to lift spray paint will lift film too. If your glass carries tint we work the exterior only and tell you in writing what that means for the result.

Will they just tag it again?

Sometimes, which is why speed matters. Glass that is cleared within a day reads as watched. Glass that sits for a month reads as abandoned. Most of our Old Town accounts moved to a monthly clean after the first removal and the tagging dropped off.

Tagged overnight?

Call before noon and it is usually gone before you close.
