Installation Notes

  • A and B must clear the Ford oval, the tailgate handle recess and the raised MAVERICK letters. Vinyl will not lie flat over any of them. Center each piece in the flat panel to its side of the oval.
  • C sits in the band below the MAVERICK letters. That band measures roughly 4.2" tall off a photo — verify on the vehicle before cutting and tell us if the bar needs to come down to 3".
  • D and E are centered on each door's flat area, clear of the handle scoop and the lower body crease. All four doors use the same 20 × 10" footprint — set every one at the same height off the rocker so the four read as a set.
  • All seven pieces are white artwork only — no plates, no backgrounds. They must read as lettering applied to the paint. Only the logo mark prints in color.
  • Artwork is supplied at exact size. Please print at 100% — do not scale to fit.
  • Outdoor-rated vinyl with UV laminate throughout. Cast film preferred over calendered.
