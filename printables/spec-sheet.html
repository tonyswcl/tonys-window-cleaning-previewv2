Installation Notes

  • A and B must clear the Ford oval, the tailgate handle recess and the raised MAVERICK letters. Vinyl will not lie flat over any of them. Centre each piece in the flat panel to its side of the oval.
  • C sits in the band below the MAVERICK letters. That band measures roughly 4.2" tall off a photo — please verify on the vehicle before cutting and tell us if the bar needs to come down to 3".
  • D and E are centred on each door's flat area, clear of the handle scoop and the lower body crease. Keep the top edge level with, or just below, the beltline trim. Match height left to right.
  • All seven pieces are white artwork only — no plates, no backgrounds. They must read as lettering applied directly to the paint. Only the logo mark prints in colour.
  • Artwork is supplied at exact size. Please print at 100% — do not scale to fit.
  • Outdoor-rated vinyl with UV laminate throughout. Cast film preferred over calendered.
