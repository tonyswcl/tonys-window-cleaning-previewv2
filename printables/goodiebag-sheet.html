How to run it

At the end of every job

  • Walk the customer around the glass first. Get the "wow" before you hand anything over.
  • Hand them the bag and say one line: "There's a thank-you in here, and two cards worth $25 off for anyone you send my way."
  • Then stop talking about it. The card does the rest.

Tracking it

  • When a referral card comes back, the Referred by line tells you who to pay.
  • Pay the $25 the day the referred job is paid — fast payment is what makes people hand out the second card.
  • Keep a simple tally. If a name shows up twice, that customer is worth a Christmas card.
Keep the review ask and the reward separate. The reward on the referral card is paid for sending a customer, never for writing a review. Google's policies prohibit incentivized reviews and will filter or remove them, and Yelp penalizes businesses that solicit reviews at all — which is why the Yelp code here is labelled "find us" rather than "review us". Asking every customer for an honest review, with nothing attached, is allowed and is what this card does.
