      FAQ

      Solar Panel Cleaning questions

      Do you cover Oak Hills?

      Yes. Oak Hills sits on our regular run between Hesperia and Phelan. We work across West Oak Hills, Country Mesa Estates, Yucca Inn, the Ranchero corridor and Baldy Mesa. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

      What does solar panel cleaning cost in Oak Hills?

      From $7 a panel, but Oak Hills arrays commonly run thirty panels or more, so expect $210 to $300 on a typical property. Ground mounted arrays are quoted separately.

      Is quarterly overkill up here?

      On the exposed parcels, no. Oak Hills gets the wind before Hesperia does and panels film over faster, so quarterly usually pays for itself in recovered output on a large array.

      Can I just hose them off?

      A hose leaves the minerals behind, which is the part that blocks light. Tap water in this region is hard, so it dries into spots and you end up worse than before. That is the whole reason we run deionized water.
