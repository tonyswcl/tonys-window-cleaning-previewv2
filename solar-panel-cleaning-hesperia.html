What is different about Hesperia

Hesperia panels carry a specific kind of film. Pass grit is angular rather than round, so it keys into the glass instead of resting on it, and a hose does almost nothing. We run the soft brush slower here than we do down the hill.

Because we are based in town, Hesperia is where we do most of our twice yearly maintenance work. If you are on the Ranchero side we are usually within a few streets of you on any given week, so fitting you in rarely takes more than a few days.

Solar Panel Cleaning questions

Do you cover Hesperia?

Yes. Hesperia is home base, not a service area we drive out to, so we are here most days of the week. We work across The Mesa, the Ranchero corridor, Hesperia Golf & Country Club, Downtown Hesperia, Summit Valley and Mesa Linda. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

What does solar panel cleaning cost in Hesperia?

A twenty panel Hesperia home runs about $140 to $160. Two story adds to it, and if the array has not been touched in over a year the first clean takes longer and we say so before we start.

How often should Hesperia panels be cleaned?

Twice a year suits most homes here, spring after the windy season and autumn before the shorter days. If you are on the Ranchero side or near open ground, quarterly is worth it because pass grit keys into the glass quickly.

Will cleaning actually change my output?

Soiling does reduce production, and out here we get almost no rain to rinse it off. We will not quote you a percentage for your specific roof because that would be guessing. Check your own production data before and after and you will see it yourself.
