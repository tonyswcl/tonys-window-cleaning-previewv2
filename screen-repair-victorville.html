Neighborhoods

What is different about Victorville

Old Town Victorville screens are frequently the original aluminum frames, decades old, and the frame corners can be as tired as the mesh. We will tell you honestly if a frame is past re-meshing rather than take your money twice.

On the newer Eagle Ranch and Brentwood tracts it is the opposite problem. The frames are fine and only the mesh has failed, which is the cheapest possible version of this job.

No trip charge anywhere in Victorville, and no charge to come out and look.

FAQ

Screen Repair questions

Do you cover Victorville?

Yes. Victorville is the biggest city on our route and we are somewhere in it most weeks. We work across Spring Valley Lake, Eagle Ranch, Brentwood, Old Town, Mojave Heights, Green Tree and Mountain View Acres. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

What does screen repair cost in Victorville?

$64.99 a screen in all weather mesh, standard $53.99. Sliding door screens run roughly double a window screen because of the size.

My frames are really old. Are they worth re-meshing?

Often yes, but not always. Old Town aluminum frames can be as tired as the mesh, and if the corners have gone we will tell you rather than re-mesh something that will fail again. That advice costs nothing.

Is there a minimum?

There is a $149 job minimum, because driving out for two screens does not cover the trip. Most people do all their screens at once, which works out better per screen anyway.
