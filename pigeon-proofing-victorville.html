What is different about Victorville

Two story is the norm on the Eagle Ranch and Bear Valley tracts, and that changes the job. Taller ladders, more setup, more time on the roof, and a genuine safety cost that gets priced in honestly rather than hidden.

Victorville arrays are also frequently split across more than one roof plane, which means more linear feet of perimeter to seal than the panel count alone suggests. We measure the edge, not the array, before quoting.
