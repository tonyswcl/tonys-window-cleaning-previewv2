What we run into in Victorville

Victorville is the biggest city in the High Desert and the houses are all over the map. Older neighborhoods, newer tracts, small lots and large ones. We quote it house by house rather than off a chart.

Two story homes are common here and we do not treat that as a special request. It is a normal day for us and we do not price it like an exception.

There is solar on a lot of roofs in the newer parts of town. Those panels lose output quietly and nothing tells you it is happening. Most people we clean for have never once opened their inverter app.

Plenty of the older homes still have their original aluminum screen frames. Those we can almost always save. We strip them and put new mesh in rather than selling you whole new screens you do not need.
