What we run into in Phelan

Phelan is rural and a lot of it is off dirt roads, so dust is constant in a way it is not in town. Screens and tracks take the worst of it.

Like Oak Hills, most of Phelan is on well water. Hard water spotting on the glass is common and sprinkler overspray is usually the cause. If there is a cloudy band low on the pane that does not wipe off, that is mineral etching and it needs restoration rather than a wash.

The wind out here is serious. It is the reason we clip our pigeon proofing mesh to the panel frame instead of bolting through anything. Bolts fail in this wind and they void panel warranties. Clips hold and they leave your roof and your warranty alone.

We come out to Phelan regularly, so being further out does not mean we cannot get to you. Give us a call and we will tell you honestly when we can be there.
