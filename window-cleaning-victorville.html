What does window cleaning cost in Victorville?

My windows look cloudy right after a wash. Why?

At Spring Valley Lake that is almost always hard water etched into the glass by mineral heavy irrigation, not dirt. A normal wash will not lift it. We will do one pane with the restoration process so you can see the difference before committing.

Why does the glass look cloudy after a normal wash?

That is usually hard water etched into the glass by sprinkler overspray, not dirt. It needs a restoration process rather than a wash. We will tell you honestly if the etching has gone too deep to fully recover.
