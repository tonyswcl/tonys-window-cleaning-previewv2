Why does my glass spot so badly up here?

Well water in this area is hard, and when it dries on glass the minerals stay behind. We wash and rinse with purified deionized water so there is nothing left to dry into a spot.

Why does the glass look cloudy after a normal wash?

That is usually hard water etched into the glass by sprinkler overspray, not dirt. It needs a restoration process rather than a wash. We will tell you honestly if the etching has gone too deep to fully recover.
