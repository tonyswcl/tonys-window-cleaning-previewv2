Why It Matters

Pigeons under solar panels aren't just noisy

The gap between your panels and the roof is warm, shaded and hidden from hawks. Out here it's the best real estate a pigeon will ever find — and once a pair nests, the flock follows.

  • ⚡ Your panels stop producing what you're paying for Droppings and nesting debris shade cells and bake on in 100°+ heat. A fouled array doesn't produce the numbers you financed it on.
  • 🧱 Droppings eat roofing and hardware Pigeon droppings are acidic. Left on tile, shingle, flashing and panel mounts, they corrode and stain — and the damage isn't reversible with a rinse.
  • 🔥 Nesting material is dry tinder next to live wiring Twigs, feathers and dried droppings packed against conduit and junction boxes are exactly what you don't want on a High Desert roof in August.
  • 🐾 It ends up in your yard, your pool and your dog Every rain and every wind moves droppings off the roof and down. Pets that get into it get sick, and pool chemistry pays for it too.
  • 😷 Dried droppings turn into airborne dust This is why we sanitize instead of sweeping, and why we bag and haul everything. Dry-brushing a fouled array just puts it in the air you breathe.
  • 📈 It gets worse, never better Pigeons return to a successful nest site and breed year-round in this climate. The job is always cheaper before the flock triples.

The Difference

Why we don't bolt mesh to your panels

We get called out constantly to redo somebody else's pigeon proofing. It's nearly always the same failure.

Bolted mesh — the job we keep redoing

  • Holes drilled through the mesh and sometimes the panel frame
  • Drilling into panel hardware can void your solar warranty
  • Wind works the mesh off the bolt heads — then it flaps and tears
  • Gaps open at the corners and the pigeons walk back in
  • Panels can't be pulled for service without cutting the mesh

Clip-secured mesh — how we do it

  • Stainless clips grip the panel frame — zero penetrations
  • Nothing drilled, so your solar warranty stays intact
  • Continuous tension around the whole perimeter, holds in wind
  • Sealed corners, no re-entry points left behind
  • Removable for panel service, then reinstalled clean
  • Backed by our 2-year pigeon-free warranty

FAQ

Pigeon proofing questions

How much does pigeon proofing cost?

Most solar array jobs start at $450 and are priced by panel count and roof access. That includes removing old nests and mesh, full dropping cleanup and sanitizing, and installing new mesh secured with clips. The inspection is free and the quote is firm before we start.

Why do you use clips instead of bolts?

Drilling bolts through the mesh puts holes in the material and, on some installs, in the panel frame — which can void your solar warranty. Worse, the mesh works itself off those bolts in High Desert wind and the pigeons walk right back in. Stainless clips grip the panel frame without penetrating it, hold in wind, and come off cleanly if a panel ever needs service.

Do you remove the droppings and nests too?

Yes — and it's included, not an upcharge. We remove the nests, bag and haul the debris, wash and sanitize the affected roof and panel area, then rinse the panels. You should never be left with cleanup after a pigeon proofing job.

Is it humane?

Yes. We don't trap, poison or harm the birds. We remove the nesting habitat, seal the gap under the array, and add deterrents so the flock relocates and stops treating your roof as home.

How long does it take?

Most residential solar arrays are finished in a single day. Larger arrays, heavy nesting or difficult roof access can run into a second day — and we tell you that at the inspection, not after we've started.

What if the pigeons come back?

Our pigeon proofing carries a 2-year pigeon-free warranty. If pigeons get back under the array within that period, we come back and fix it at no charge.
