Your panels, your roof, your spinners

What spinners cover, and what they don't

They do
  • Flash sunlight and keep moving in the wind. Pigeons stay away from light and motion they can't predict.
  • Keep birds off the open roof, the ridge and the eaves, where they land and walk.
  • Cover the side of the roof they face. The flash travels in a straight line.
  • Mount on your roof vents with stainless hose clamps. No drilling into the roof.
They don't
  • Clear a nest that's already under your panels. The mesh does that.
  • See over the ridge. One spinner on the front leaves the back side open, and the pigeons just move there.
  • Replace the mesh. Mesh is the guarantee, spinners are the deterrent.

FAQ

Review questions

Are these real reviews?

Yes. Every one is copied from our Google Business Profile as written, spelling included. The Google listing has all 25 and you can read them there without taking our word for it.

Do you pay or discount for reviews?

No. We ask every customer once after the job, by text, with the link. That is it. Paid or incentivized reviews break Google's rules and would put the listing at risk, which is worth more to us than any single review.

Why only 25 reviews if you have been doing this a while?

Because for a long time we did not ask. Most of the work came from referrals and repeat customers and nobody thought to send the link. We ask now, every job, and the count moves every month.
