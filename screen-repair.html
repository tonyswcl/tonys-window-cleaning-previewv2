Why Bother

A torn screen isn't a small thing

Between UV exposure that runs most of the year, wind-driven grit and the swing between a summer afternoon and a winter night, mesh goes brittle and frames warp faster out here than almost anywhere. Most people put up with torn screens for years because it seems too small to call someone about. Here's what it's actually costing you:

  • 🌪️ Dust goes straight into the house A torn screen is an open door for High Desert grit — more cleaning inside, dirtier windows outside.
  • 🔊 Sagging screens rattle in the wind Bad tension turns every windy afternoon into a percussion section on your window frames.
  • 🏠 It reads as neglect from the street Visible tears make a house look worn down even when everything else is spotless.
On Site, Done Right

We re-mesh at your house

We're not taking your screens away and bringing them back in a week, and you're not driving them anywhere.

Strip & inspect

The frame gets stripped, cleaned and checked. If it's bent, corroded or broken at the corners, we say so rather than putting new mesh on something that will fail.

Cut & tension

New mesh cut and tensioned properly. Tension is what separates a repair that lasts from one that ripples and sags within a season — and it's the part DIY kits get wrong.

Seat & reinstall

Fresh spline seated, screen reinstalled. About 15–20 minutes per screen once we're set up. Most homes finish in one visit.

Mesh Options

Pick the right mesh for the window

  • 🪟 Standard fiberglass — charcoal black Best visibility from inside, good airflow, the right choice for most windows. Charcoal disappears visually far better than the older grey mesh.
  • 🐾 Pet-proof — about 7× stronger Heavy vinyl-coated polyester. If you have a cat that climbs screens or a dog that pushes at them, this ends the problem permanently. Worth it on any door or low window a pet can reach.
  • ☀️ Solar screen mesh — blocks heat and glare On west- and south-facing windows it makes a real difference to how a room feels in August, and it takes load off the AC. It darkens the view somewhat — that's the tradeoff.
  • 🔧 New frames when the old one is done Bent, corroded or broken at the corners? Sometimes a frame isn't worth re-meshing — we'll say so and quote a replacement instead.

FAQ

Screen repair questions

How much does screen repair cost?

Standard mesh re-screening starts at $33.99 per screen, pet-proof at $46.99. New frames and solar mesh are quoted by size. There's a $149 job minimum — which is why most people do all their screens at once.

Can you repair just a small tear?

Usually not worth it. A patch is visible, it doesn't hold tension, and the surrounding mesh is the same age. Re-meshing the whole screen costs little more and looks correct.

How long does each screen take?

Around 15–20 minutes once we're set up. Most homes are done in a single visit — at your house, not a shop.

Do you make screens for sliding doors?

Yes, including pet-proof mesh — the most common request for doors.

What about sun-damaged frames?

We inspect and tell you honestly. If a frame is too far gone, we quote a replacement rather than putting good mesh on a bad frame.

Do I need to remove them first?

No. We handle removal, repair and reinstallation.
