Why Bother

A torn screen isn't a small thing

Between UV exposure that runs most of the year, wind-driven grit and the swing between a summer afternoon and a winter night, mesh goes brittle and frames warp faster out here than almost anywhere. Most people put up with torn screens for years because it seems too small to call someone about. Here's what it's actually costing you:

  • 🌪️ Dust goes straight into the house A torn screen is an open door for High Desert grit. More cleaning inside, dirtier windows outside.
  • 🔊 Sagging screens rattle in the wind Bad tension turns every windy afternoon into a percussion section on your window frames.
  • 🏠 It reads as neglect from the street Visible tears make a house look worn down even when everything else is spotless.
On Site, Done Right

We re-mesh at your house

We're not taking your screens away and bringing them back in a week, and you're not driving them anywhere.

Strip & inspect

The frame gets stripped, cleaned and checked. If it's bent, corroded or broken at the corners, we say so rather than putting new mesh on something that will fail.

Cut & tension

New mesh cut and tensioned properly. Tension is what separates a repair that lasts from one that ripples and sags within a season, and it's the part DIY kits get wrong.

Seat & reinstall

Fresh spline seated, screen reinstalled. About 15–20 minutes per screen once we're set up. Most homes finish in one visit.

Mesh Options

Pick the right mesh for the window

Almost every production home up here went up with standard charcoal fiberglass. It is the cheapest mesh that passes inspection, and it holds up fine somewhere mild. Out here the sun runs UV on it nine months a year until it goes brittle, then the wind arrives with sand in it and tears what is left. Most people replace those screens two or three times before anyone tells them the material was wrong from the start.

  • 🛡️ All weather pet mesh, $64.99 per screen, what we recommend Heavy vinyl-coated polyester, roughly seven times stronger than the builder mesh and far more UV stable. It takes desert sun and wind without going brittle, and it takes a dog or a cat leaning on it without blowing out. This is the one that stops you replacing screens every couple of years.
  • 🪟 Standard fiberglass, charcoal, $53.99 per screen Clearest view from inside, and good airflow. The same class of mesh your builder used, installed properly and tensioned right. Fine for shaded windows and anywhere the wind does not hit.
  • ☀️ Solar screen mesh, blocks heat and glare On west and south facing windows it makes a real difference to how a room feels in August, and it takes load off the AC. It darkens the view somewhat, that is the tradeoff. Quoted by size.
  • 🚪 Sliding doors, done on site Most shops send sliding door screens out. We re-mesh them in your driveway with the rest. Priced by size since door screens run roughly double a window.
  • 🔧 New frames when the old one is done Bent, corroded or broken at the corners? Sometimes a frame is not worth re-meshing. We will say so and quote a replacement instead of charging you for work that will not last.

FAQ

Screen repair questions

How much does screen repair cost?

All weather mesh is $64.99 per screen. Standard mesh is $53.99. Sliding doors run roughly double a window. New frames and solar mesh are quoted by size. There's a $149 job minimum, which is why most people do all their screens at once.

Can you repair just a small tear?

Usually not worth it. A patch is visible, it doesn't hold tension, and the surrounding mesh is the same age. Re-meshing the whole screen costs little more and looks correct.

How long does each screen take?

Around 15–20 minutes once we're set up. Most homes are done in a single visit, at your house rather than a shop.

Do you make screens for sliding doors?

Yes, including pet-proof mesh, which is the most common request for doors.

What about sun damaged frames?

We inspect and tell you honestly. If a frame is too far gone, we quote a replacement rather than putting good mesh on a bad frame.

Do I need to remove the screens first?

No. We handle removal, repair and reinstallation.
