What is different about Adelanto

Builder grade mesh across the newer Adelanto tracts is failing on schedule, whole blocks reaching the end of it within a year or two of each other. We have re-meshed houses out here barely five years old.

FAQ

Screen Repair questions

Do you cover Adelanto?

Yes. Adelanto is a regular stop, El Mirage included. We work across El Mirage, Mojave Heights, the Koala Road area and the newer tracts off Rancho Road. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

What does screen repair cost in Adelanto?

$55.99 a screen in all weather mesh through September 30, then $64.99. Standard is $53.99, but given what the dry lake dust does to thin mesh, the all weather material is the one worth buying here.

My house is only five years old. Why are the screens going?

Builders fit the cheapest mesh that passes inspection. It is thin, it has almost no UV package, and out here it starts going chalky around year three. Re-meshing fixes it and you keep the frames you already have.

Do you take the screens away?

No. We strip, re-mesh and re-tension on site, usually in one visit. You are never left with open windows waiting on a shop.
