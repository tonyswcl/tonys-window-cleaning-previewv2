What is different about Hesperia

Hesperia glass has two separate problems and they need different treatments. There is the grit film, which comes off with a normal purified water clean, and there is sprinkler etching, which does not. On the golf course streets you usually have both.

Tracks are the part people underestimate here. We vacuum them out and detail them on every exterior clean, because a track full of pass sand puts that grit straight back onto clean glass the first time you open a window.

Do you cover Hesperia?

Yes. Hesperia is home base, not a service area we drive out to, so we are here most days of the week. We work across The Mesa, the Ranchero corridor, Hesperia Golf & Country Club, Downtown Hesperia, Summit Valley and Mesa Linda. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

What does window cleaning cost in Hesperia?

Single story exterior starts at $149 and two story at $249, with screens, tracks and sills in that number. Hard water restoration is quoted separately after we see how deep the etching has gone.

Why do my tracks fill with sand so fast?

The Cajon Pass funnels wind straight into town carrying fine grit, and it gets past weatherstripping into the track. We vacuum and detail the tracks on every exterior clean at no extra charge, because leaving them undoes the glass.

Are screens and tracks really included?

Yes, in every exterior clean. Leaving grit in a screen undoes the glass within a week, so charging separately for it never made sense to us.
