How it runs

What a Victorville account looks like

Victorville storefronts get more foot traffic than anywhere else up here, which means fingerprints, push plate smears and dust film building faster than a monthly visit can handle on a busy door. Most of our Bear Valley and Old Town accounts run biweekly.

We work after close. For a barbershop that is Sunday evening. For a restaurant it is after the last table. For an office it is any weeknight after five. You leave a key, a code, or a staff member, and open the next morning to finished glass.

Old Town glass often needs a first visit that is more than a clean. Years of sticker adhesive, old vinyl lettering and tag residue come off under adhesive removal or graffiti removal, and after that the monthly visit keeps it clear.

How much is storefront window cleaning in Victorville?

From $89 a visit for storefront glass inside and out, up to ten pane faces, on a monthly schedule. Biweekly is $79 a visit and weekly is $69. Interior partitions and mirrors add $50. Bear Valley Road centers with several units are priced per unit and billed to one manager if you prefer.

Can you come after we close?

That is how nearly every Victorville account runs. Restaurants after the last table, salons and barbershops Sunday evening, offices any weeknight. No customer walks across a wet floor and no appointment time is lost.

Our storefront gets tagged. Is that a separate service?

Yes, and it is the reason a lot of Old Town businesses call us the first time. Graffiti removal starts at $129 and is usually same day. Once it is clear, the recurring clean keeps the glass looking watched, which is the best deterrent there is. See the Victorville graffiti page.
