What we run into in Hesperia

Hesperia is where we are based, so most weeks we are somewhere between Main Street and Ranchero Road already. If you are in town we can usually get to you fast, and a lot of the time we can swing by the same week you call.

The wind coming up out of the Cajon Pass is the thing that makes Hesperia different. It carries fine grit and it drives it into everything. Screens go brittle, window tracks pack solid with sand, and solar panels get a film on them that rain never fully takes off because we barely get rain.

A lot of the older tracts off Main and Bear Valley have original screens on them that have never been touched. Those are the ones that tear as soon as you look at them. We re mesh those on site so you are not without screens for a week.

Pigeons are a real problem here on anything with solar. The gap under the panels is warm and shaded and out of the wind, which is exactly what they want in a place with this much open desert around it.
