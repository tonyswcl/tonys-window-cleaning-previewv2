Your panels, your roof, your spinners

What spinners cover, and what they don't

They do
  • Flash sunlight and keep moving in the wind. Pigeons stay away from light and motion they can't predict.
  • Keep birds off the open roof, the ridge and the eaves, where they land and walk.
  • Cover the side of the roof they face. The flash travels in a straight line.
  • Mount on your roof vents with stainless hose clamps. No drilling into the roof.
They don't
  • Clear a nest that's already under your panels. The mesh does that.
  • See over the ridge. One spinner on the front leaves the back side open, and the pigeons just move there.
  • Replace the mesh. Mesh is the guarantee, spinners are the deterrent.

Further out

Places we reach on a planned day

Barstow and Lucerne Valley get a visit most weeks. We group jobs so the drive is shared and the price stays on the list.

Wrightwood, Pinon Hills, Baldy Mesa and Summit Valley are mountain and high plateau work. Cooler, fewer pigeons, a lot more pine debris in gutters and on panels.

Helendale, Silver Lakes and Spring Valley Lake share one thing: lake or well irrigation that leaves mineral deposits on every low pane. If you are in any of these, read the hard water page before you call, because a standard wash will not fix what you are looking at.

Inland Empire

Down the hill, on request

We started with Inland Empire customers and still take the calls. Fontana, Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario, San Bernardino, Rialto, Redlands and Upland all get solar and pigeon work from us, usually batched onto one day.

The IE is a different market. Denser tracts, more HOA rules about what can be visible on a roof, and less hard water than the desert. We are writing dedicated pages for it. Until then, call and ask what day we are already down there and you will get the same price list as everyone else.

FAQ

Service area questions

Do you charge a trip fee to come out?

Not inside the six core cities. Hesperia, Victorville, Apple Valley, Adelanto, Oak Hills and Phelan are all priced from the same list with nothing added for distance. Further out, Barstow, Lucerne Valley, Wrightwood and Helendale, we usually pair the drive with another job and tell you up front if anything is added.

Is the price the same in every city?

Yes. What changes the number is the house, not the zip code. Single or two story, how many panels, how long the pigeons have been there, whether the glass has hard water etching. Each city page explains what we usually run into in that town, but the starting prices on this page apply everywhere.

Do you still work in the Inland Empire?

Yes, on request. Fontana, Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario, San Bernardino, Rialto and Redlands all get regular visits, mostly solar and pigeon work. We are building out proper pages for the IE next. For now, call and we will tell you what day we are already down the hill.

What if my city is not listed?

Call anyway. Silver Lakes, Spring Valley Lake, Pinon Hills, Baldy Mesa, Summit Valley and Lake Arrowhead are all places we have worked. If it is within a reasonable drive of Hesperia we can almost always make it work, and we will say so honestly if we cannot.

Why do you have a separate page for each city?

Because the work is different. Spring Valley Lake glass is etched by lake irrigation, Adelanto arrays are dusted by the dry lake, Phelan homes are on well water and spread out on big lots. A page that treats them all the same would be lying to somebody. Each city page carries real photos from jobs in that city.

Not sure if we reach you?

Call and ask. If it is within a fair drive of Hesperia the answer is almost always yes.
