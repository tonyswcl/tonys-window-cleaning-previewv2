Neighborhoods

What is different about Apple Valley

Sliding door screens are the request we get most in the Apple Valley 55+ communities, because they get used every day and they are the heaviest thing on the house to handle. Those get all weather mesh as a matter of course.

We re-mesh at the property so nothing has to be carried anywhere, which matters more here than it does elsewhere. You are not lifting a patio door screen into a car.

No trip charge anywhere in Apple Valley, and no charge to come out and look.

FAQ

Screen Repair questions

Do you cover Apple Valley?

Yes. Apple Valley is a regular stop, including inside the gated 55+ communities. We work across Jess Ranch, Sun City, Solera at Del Webb, Desert Knolls and Bell Mountain Estates. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

What does screen repair cost in Apple Valley?

$64.99 a screen in all weather mesh. Sliding doors are roughly double a window screen. $149 job minimum, which most homes clear easily.

Can you do patio door screens?

Yes, and they are the most common request we get in the 55+ communities here. They get all weather mesh as standard because of how much they get used.

Do you take the screens away?

No. We strip, re-mesh and re-tension on site, usually in one visit. You are never left with open windows waiting on a shop.
