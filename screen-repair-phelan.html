What is different about Phelan

At this elevation the daily temperature swing is severe, and that constant expansion and contraction is what finally works standard mesh loose and splits it. Screens fail years earlier in Phelan than they do in Victorville.

For that reason we fit all weather mesh as the default up here rather than offering it as an upgrade. On a Phelan house the standard material is genuinely not worth the saving.
