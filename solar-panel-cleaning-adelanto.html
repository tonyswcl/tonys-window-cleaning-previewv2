What is different about Adelanto

El Mirage dust is fine, alkaline and abrasive, and it bonds to panel glass harder than ordinary road dust. Off road traffic keeps it airborne, so Adelanto panels need attention more often than the valley average.

Solar came as standard on most of this construction, which means a lot of Adelanto owners have arrays they have never thought about. If you have never checked your inverter app, this is the city where that matters most.
