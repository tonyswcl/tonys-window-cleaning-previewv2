Your panels, your roof, your spinners

What spinners cover, and what they don't

They do
  • Flash sunlight and keep moving in the wind. Pigeons stay away from light and motion they can't predict.
  • Keep birds off the open roof, the ridge and the eaves, where they land and walk.
  • Cover the side of the roof they face. The flash travels in a straight line.
  • Mount on your roof vents with stainless hose clamps. No drilling into the roof.
They don't
  • Clear a nest that's already under your panels. The mesh does that.
  • See over the ridge. One spinner on the front leaves the back side open, and the pigeons just move there.
  • Replace the mesh. Mesh is the guarantee, spinners are the deterrent.
Oak Hills

Big lots, big arrays, and wind straight off the pass

Oak Hills is unincorporated and it is one of the more expensive parts of the Victor Valley, mostly because of the multi-acre lots. That acreage is exactly what changes this job.

Bigger parcels mean bigger houses, longer driveways and noticeably larger solar arrays than the tract neighborhoods down the hill. More panel edge to seal means more material and more labor when birds get under there, and we price off that footage rather than off the square footage of the house.

Sitting right above the pass means Oak Hills gets the wind before Hesperia does. Panels film over quickly, screens go brittle early, and anything not fastened down properly starts working itself loose.

A lot of properties out here are on wells, and well water in this area is hard. It shows up as mineral spotting on glass that will not come off with a normal wash, which is why we finish everything with purified water instead.

FAQ

Oak Hills questions

My lot is a few acres. Does that change the price?

Not by itself. We price roof work off how much panel edge and roof perimeter we have to seal, and window work off how many windows you actually have. Acreage only matters if it means a longer equipment run or more outbuildings.

Why does my glass spot even after it is cleaned?

If you are on a well, that is minerals in the water drying on the glass. We rinse and finish with purified deionized water, which has the minerals stripped out, so it dries clear instead of spotting.

How long does a big Oak Hills array take to proof?

Usually a full day, sometimes two if the nesting is years deep. We tell you which on the free inspection, and the number does not move after that.

Do you charge extra to come out to Oak Hills?

No. Oak Hills is on our regular route between Hesperia and Phelan. There is no trip charge and no charge for the quote.
