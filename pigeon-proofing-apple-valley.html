Do you drill into the roof or the panels?

No. We clip the mesh to the panel frame. Bolting through mesh puts holes in the material and sometimes the frame, which can void your solar warranty, and out here the wind works it loose off the bolt heads within a couple of seasons. We get called to redo that job often.
