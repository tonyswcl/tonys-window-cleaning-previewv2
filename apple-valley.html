      Apple Valley

      Half of Apple Valley should not be on a ladder

      Apple Valley has roughly five thousand age-restricted homes across its 55+ communities, and that changes what this job actually is. A lot of our customers here are not deciding whether to hire someone, they are deciding who. Getting up a ladder is not on the table.

      Jess Ranch alone is over two thousand homes, and Sun City and Solera add most of another two thousand. These are well-kept properties where the owners simply cannot do the roof and gutter work themselves anymore, and they want somebody who shows up when he says he will and does not surprise them with a bill.

      Desert Knolls is the opposite end of the town. Bigger lots, older houses, more trees, and gutters that fill with debris and then start attracting nesting.

      Out toward Bell Mountain the properties get rural and exposed. More wind, more dust on the panels, and longer runs of roof edge to seal when the pigeons find it.
