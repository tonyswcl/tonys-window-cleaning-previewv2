FAQ

Pigeon Proofing questions

Do you cover Oak Hills?

Yes. Oak Hills sits on our regular run between Hesperia and Phelan. We work across West Oak Hills, Country Mesa Estates, Yucca Inn, the Ranchero corridor and Baldy Mesa. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

What does pigeon proofing cost in Oak Hills?

Because of array size, Oak Hills pigeon work commonly runs $700 to $1,100 rather than the $450 start. We measure the panel perimeter and give you the real number before anything begins.

Does my acreage change the price?

Not by itself. We price off how much panel edge and roof perimeter we have to seal. Acreage only matters if it means a longer equipment run or additional structures the birds are using.

Is the cleanup included or extra?

Included. Nests, eggs and droppings are cleared, bagged and hauled off, and the area is washed and sanitized before any mesh goes on. You should never be handed a broom at the end of a pigeon job.
