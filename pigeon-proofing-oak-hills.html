    What spinners cover, and what they don't

    They do
    • Flash sunlight and keep moving in the wind. Pigeons stay away from light and motion they can't predict.
    • Keep birds off the open roof, the ridge and the eaves, where they land and walk.
    • Cover the side of the roof they face. The flash travels in a straight line.
    • Mount on your roof vents with stainless hose clamps. No drilling into the roof.
    They don't
    • Clear a nest that's already under your panels. The cleanout and the mesh do that.
    • See over the ridge. One spinner on the front leaves the back side open, and the pigeons just move there.
    • Replace the mesh. Mesh is the guarantee, spinners are the deterrent.

    FAQ

    Pigeon Proofing questions

    Do you cover Oak Hills?

    Yes. Oak Hills sits on our regular run between Hesperia and Phelan. We work across West Oak Hills, Country Mesa Estates, Yucca Inn, the Ranchero corridor and Baldy Mesa. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

    What does pigeon proofing cost in Oak Hills?

    Because of array size, Oak Hills pigeon work commonly runs $700 to $1,100 rather than the $450 start. We measure the panel perimeter and give you the real number before anything begins.

    Do you install bird spinners?

    Yes, and they are free. Every pigeon proofing job includes 2 to 3 reflective spinners, depending on the size of the home. They flash and turn in the wind and make the open roof less inviting to land on, which takes pressure off the mesh. The mesh keeps pigeons out from under the panels; the spinners make them less likely to land on the roof at all.

    Does my acreage change the price?

    Not by itself. We price off how much panel edge and roof perimeter we have to seal. Acreage only matters if it means a longer equipment run or additional structures the birds are using.

    Is the cleanup included or extra?

    Included. Nests, eggs and droppings are cleared, bagged and hauled off, and the area is washed and sanitized before any mesh goes on. You should never be handed a broom at the end of a pigeon job.
