FAQ

Commercial window cleaning questions

How much is commercial window cleaning in the High Desert?

Storefront glass starts at $89 a visit for up to ten pane faces inside and out. The rate drops the more often we come: $79 biweekly and $69 weekly. Interior glass, partitions and mirrors are $50 a visit on top. Bigger buildings are quoted on site and the price is firm before we start.

Do you work after hours?

Yes, and for most of our commercial accounts that is the point. We come after close so no appointment time is lost, no customer walks across a wet floor, and the glass is finished before you open. Barbershops, restaurants and medical offices are almost always done in the evening.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Every recurring account is month to month with seven days notice to stop. There is no cancellation fee and nothing owed beyond visits already completed. We would rather keep you because the glass looks right than because you are locked in.

Can you do one invoice a month instead of per visit?

Yes. Recurring accounts get a single invoice at the end of the month covering that month's visits, due on receipt. It keeps your bookkeeping to one line instead of four.

Are you insured, and can you send a certificate?

Yes. General liability coverage is in place and we send a certificate on request before the first visit. Most property managers ask for it and we have it ready.

Do you handle storefronts with old signage or graffiti?

Yes. Adhesive, old vinyl lettering and graffiti are separate services we do regularly, and they are commonly the reason a business calls us the first time. See our graffiti removal and adhesive removal pages.

Need it handled this week?

Call or text and we will come look at it. Free assessment, firm price before any work starts.
